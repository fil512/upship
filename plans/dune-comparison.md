### Dune Imperium Market Deck - Cost Distribution (~67 cards)

Exact numbers unavailable, but from strategy discussions:
- Cards range from 1-9 cost
- Reserve cards always available: Arrakis Liaison (cost 2), The Spice Must Flow (cost 9)
- Players report the market often clogs with 1-2 cost cards nobody wants
- Most desirable cards are in 4-6 cost range
- Low-cost cards (1-2) are generally considered weak/deck-cloggers

### Reserve Card (Always Available)

Like Dune Imperium's **Arrakis Liaison**, UP SHIP! now has a reserve card:

## Key Observations

### 1. ~~UP SHIP! Has More "Dead" Influence Cards~~ **FIXED**
- ~~50% of starting deck gives 0 influence vs 30% in Dune Imperium~~ → Now 30% (matches Dune)
- ~~43% of market deck gives 0 influence~~ → Now 30% (matches Dune)
- These cards still provide other valuable resources (engineers, officers, research, cash, gas)

### 2. ~~UP SHIP! Has Higher Variance in Starting Deck~~ **FIXED**
- ~~Highest card: 3 influence (Investor)~~ → Now 2 influence (matches Dune's max of 2)
- Range is now 0-2, same as Dune Imperium
- Variance reduced to match Dune's design

### 3. Both Games Now Match: ~0.9 Currency Per Starting Card
- UP SHIP! now exactly matches Dune's 0.9/card average
- Both games let you reliably buy 2-cost cards early

### 4. UP SHIP! Market Cards Are More Expensive (No 1-Cost)
- Range: 2-6 influence
- No 1-cost cards (which Dune players often avoid anyway)
- Prevents the "clogged market" problem Dune experiences
- **NEW: Reserve card (cost 2) provides reliable upgrade path**

### 5. Buying Power Calculation (UPDATED)

**Dune Imperium (5-card hand):**
- Average hand: ~4-5 persuasion (slightly below 5 due to 0s)
- Max possible: 5 persuasion (all 1s) or higher with 2s
- After playing agents, typically 2-3 cards revealed

**UP SHIP! (5-card hand, UPDATED):**
- Average hand: ~4.5 influence (now matches Dune)
- Variance reduced: typical range 2-6 influence
- After playing agents, typically 2-4 cards revealed

## Design Implications

### What Dune Does Well (NOW MATCHED)
1. ✅ Consistent reveal values (most cards give 1 persuasion) → UP SHIP! now matches
2. ✅ Clear "upgrade path" → Reserve card provides this
3. ✅ Reserve cards (Arrakis Liaison at 2 cost) → Airship Enthusiast added

### ~~What UP SHIP! Could Consider~~ **ALL IMPLEMENTED**
1. ✅ **More consistent influence in starting deck** - Now 30% give 0 (was 50%)
2. ✅ **A cheap "always available" card** - Airship Enthusiast (cost 2, reveal 2)
3. ✅ **Reduce variance** - Range now 0-2 (was 0-3)
4. ✅ **Balance zero-influence market cards** - Now 30% (was 43%)

### Changes Implemented
1. ✅ Investor: 3 → 2 influence (reduces variance)
2. ✅ Mechanic: 0 → 1 influence (now gives 1£ + 1 influence)
3. ✅ Clerk: 0 → 1 influence (now gives 1£ + 1 influence)
4. ✅ Reserve card: Airship Enthusiast (cost 2, Any symbol, reveal 2 influence)
5. ✅ Market cards: +1 influence to Engine Specialist, Ground Crew Chief, Fuel Specialist, Foreign Consultant
