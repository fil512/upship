# UP SHIP! Rules Review

*Review date: 2025-12-29*

---

## Overview

This review examines the UP SHIP! rulebook (upship_rules.md and appendix.md) for clarity, completeness, balance, and playability. The game has a strong thematic foundation and elegant core mechanics (Physics Check, dual-use cards, Engineer tension), but several areas need attention before the rulebook is ready for blind playtesting.

**Summary of Findings:**
- 3 Critical issues (game-breaking or blocking play)
- 7 Important issues (cause confusion but don't break the game)
- 8 Moderate issues (polish items for clarity)
- 5 Low priority issues (minor completeness items)
- Several balance observations requiring playtesting

---

## Critical Issues

### Britain's Flaw is Undefined

**Location:** Section 13.2 (Britain faction)

**Problem:** Britain's flaw is listed as "**The Flaw** — Red Tape: TODO TBD flaw" with no mechanical definition. This means one of four factions has no balancing constraint, making faction selection unbalanced during playtesting.

**Remedy:** Define Britain's flaw. Thematic options include:
- **Bureaucratic Delays:** Must pay £1 extra per launch (red tape overhead)
- **Committee Design:** First upgrade installation each Age costs double
- **Imperial Obligations:** Must claim British Territory routes first when available
- **Slow Procurement:** −1 Research when Revealing (committees slow innovation)

**Priority:** Critical — cannot playtest faction balance without this.

---

### Hazard Deck Card Count Inconsistency

**Location:** Section 2.1 (Personal Hazard Deck: 24 cards), Appendix D/E (Hazard Deck: 20 cards)

**Problem:** The rules state each player has a 24-card Personal Hazard Deck, but Appendix E lists only 20 cards with a distribution of:
- Clear Weather: 4
- Minor Hazards: 8
- Major Hazards: 6
- Fire Hazards: 5
- Mechanical Hazards: 1

Total: 24 cards stated but only 20 cards specified.

**Remedy:** Either:
1. Add 4 more hazard cards to reach 24, or
2. Change Section 2.1 to state "20 cards" and update Appendix D header

**Priority:** Critical — component counts must match for physical production.

---

### Age I Network Connectivity Rule Incomplete

**Location:** Section 14.3 (Network Connectivity)

**Problem:** The rule states "Age I: Any route; but pay extra for new network" but doesn't specify:
- What does "new network" mean in Age I?
- How much extra must be paid?
- Is this per route or per disconnected network?

**Remedy:** Either:
1. Remove the network fee concept from Age I (simplifying the pioneer era), or
2. Define the rule completely: "If your new route doesn't connect to any route you already own, pay £X extra" and specify X.

**Priority:** Critical — players cannot execute launches without knowing the cost.

## Important Issues

### Aborted Launch Outcome Contradiction

**Location:** Section 8.1 (Launch Outcomes table) vs. Section 14.2 (Launch Outcomes Summary)

**Problem:** Section 8.1 states that when a launch is Aborted, "Officers: Kept" and Section 14.2 confirms this. However, Appendix B (Quick Reference) Step 12 states "**Aborted:** Ship returns to Launch Hangar (Officers and gas lost)."

The main rules say Officers are kept, but the Quick Reference says they're lost.

**Remedy:** Clarify which is correct and update inconsistent locations. The main rules logic (Officers kept because mission didn't complete) seems more thematically appropriate.

**Priority:** Important — core gameplay outcome is ambiguous.

---

### Damaged Ship Officer Outcome Contradiction

**Location:** Section 8.1 vs. Section 8.4 vs. Section 14.2

**Problem:**
- Section 8.1 table: Damaged → Officers "Kept"
- Section 8.4: "Officers return to shared supply"
- Section 14.2: Damaged → Officers "Spent"

Three different statements about what happens to Officers when a ship is damaged.

**Remedy:** Decide the correct rule and update all references. The section 8.4 text ("return to shared supply") suggests Officers are spent, which seems thematically appropriate (the officers survived but are traumatized/unavailable).

**Priority:** Important — core gameplay outcome contradicts itself.

---

### Design Bureau Swap Limit Contradiction

**Location:** Section 6.2 (Design Bureau)

**Problem:** The rules state "Limit: install or remove any number of tech tiles" but Section 13.4 states Italy has "4 tile swaps at Design Bureau instead of 2" and the Modular Frame upgrade grants "+2 tile swaps at Design Bureau."

This implies there IS a base limit (2 swaps), but Section 6.2 says "any number."

**Remedy:** Clarify the actual limit. Options:
1. Base limit of 2 swaps, Italy gets 4, Modular Frame adds +2
2. Unlimited swaps for everyone, making Italy's ability and Modular Frame meaningless (unlikely intent)

**Priority:** Important — faction ability depends on this.

---

### Income Minus Engineers Formula Unclear

**Location:** Section 5.2 (Income & Cleanup)

**Problem:** "Gain £ equal to your Income Track minus Engineers in Barracks" is unclear about whether this means:
- Engineers currently in Barracks reduces income (upkeep cost)
- Or something else entirely

The appendix clarifies this is upkeep, but the main rule doesn't explain WHY engineers reduce income.

**Remedy:** Add a clarifying sentence: "Engineers in your Barracks consume £1 each as upkeep, reducing your net income."

**Priority:** Important — affects fundamental economic planning.

---

### Third Agent Trigger Mentioned Twice Differently

**Location:** Section 5.1 (Earning Your Third Agent) vs. Section 6.6 (Flight School)

**Problem:** Both locations explain that the 3rd Agent is earned when Officer Income Track reaches +3. This duplication risks drift if one is updated without the other.

**Remedy:** State the rule once and cross-reference. Suggestion: Keep detailed explanation in Section 5.1, make Section 6.6 say "see Section 5.1 for 3rd Agent milestone."

**Priority:** Important — dual definitions risk inconsistency over time.

---

### Reveal Phase R&D Board Refill Timing

**Location:** Section 5.1 (Option 2: Reveal), Section 9.1 (The R&D Board)

**Problem:** Section 5.1 step 5 says "Replenish Markets: Refill the R&D Board and Market Row to their full capacity" immediately after acquiring Technologies. But Section 9.1 says "Draw tiles to fill the board after each player's Reveal turn."

These appear consistent but could cause confusion about whether refill happens before or after purchasing cards (step 4 is before refill in the list).

**Remedy:** Clarify the order: Is it Acquire Tech → Purchase Cards → Refill? Or Acquire Tech → Refill → Purchase Cards? The current ordering suggests the former.

**Priority:** Important — turn order affects available options.

---

### Starter Deck "Any" Cards Count

**Location:** Section 11.3 (Starter Deck)

**Problem:** The starter deck includes 2 "Apprentice" cards with "Any" symbol, allowing players to visit any location. Combined with symbol distribution across other cards, players need to verify whether 10 cards provide enough coverage for all locations.

Current distribution:
- Any: 2 (Apprentice)
- Wrench: 4 (2 Mechanic + 2 Draftsman)
- Propeller: 3 (2 Researcher + 1 Helmsman)
- Coin: 1 (Purser)

With only 1 Coin card + 2 Any cards, Coin locations (Academy, Flight School, Government Liaison, Insurance Bureau) are hard to access early.

**Remedy:** This may be intentional design, but document whether this scarcity is intended. Consider adding a second Purser or another Coin card if playtesting shows Coin locations are inaccessible.

**Priority:** Important — starting hand composition affects early game options.

## Moderate Issues

### Technology Track Names Mismatch

**Location:** Section 4.1 (Drawing Office) vs. Appendix C

**Problem:** Section 4.1 lists four tracks: Propulsion, Structure, Gas Systems, Payload. But Appendix C lists: Propulsion, Frame, Fabric, Gas Systems, Payload (5 categories, and "Structure" doesn't appear).

**Remedy:** Align terminology. Either:
1. Combine Frame and Fabric into "Structure" as one track, or
2. Update Section 4.1 to list all 5 tracks correctly

**Priority:** Moderate — organizational confusion but doesn't affect play.

---

### Section 6.5 Card Symbol Lists Inconsistent

**Location:** Section 5.1 (Card Symbols) vs. Section 6 headers

**Problem:** Section 5.1 states Technical Institute requires a Wrench symbol, but Section 6.7 (Technical Institute) is listed as (Wrench) which is correct. However, the consolidated list in Section 5.1 includes "Gas Depot, Technical Institute" but not "Construction Hall" for Wrench locations.

Actually reviewing: Section 5.1 lists Wrench as "Design Bureau, Construction Hall, Gas Depot, Technical Institute" which is 4 locations. Section 6 headers show:
- Design Bureau (Wrench)
- Construction Hall (Wrench)
- Gas Depot (Wrench)
- Technical Institute (Wrench)

This appears correct, but the Appendix A Quick Reference at the end says:
"| Wrench | Design Bureau, Construction Hall, Gas Depot, Technical Institute |"

All consistent. No issue found on deeper review.

**Remedy:** None needed — false alarm on detailed check.

**Priority:** N/A — removed.

**Location:** Section 3.2 (Blueprint Stats by Age) vs. Section 4.2

**Problem:** Section 3.2 references "Payload" slots, but earlier in player setup (Section 3.2 line about blueprint) mentions "componentSlots" in implementation. This is likely a code vs. rules mismatch rather than a rules issue.

**Remedy:** Ensure rules consistently use "Payload" for this slot type (which they do in the rules themselves).

### Age II Map Reference Missing

**Location:** Section 10.5

**Problem:** Age II says "No Map Routes" and contracts replace routes, but the Components section (2.2) lists "2 Map Boards (Age I: Western Europe, Age III: The Atlantic)" with no Age II map.

This is intentional (contracts replace map routes) but could confuse players expecting a third map.

**Remedy:** Add a note in Section 2.2: "Note: Age II uses Military Contracts instead of a map board."

**Priority:** Moderate — potential setup confusion.

### Helium Requirement Stated Twice

**Location:** Section 9.3 and Section 4.5

**Problem:** The Helium Handling technology requirement is stated in both locations. While not contradictory, this duplication increases maintenance burden.

**Remedy:** State once with cross-reference. Section 4.5 could say "Helium requires the Helium Handling technology (see Section 9.3)."

**Priority:** Moderate — documentation efficiency.

### Card Symbol Icon Definitions Incomplete

**Location:** Section 11.1 (Card Anatomy)

**Problem:** Section 11.1 mentions "Action Symbol" but doesn't show what the icons look like. Appendix A.1 (TODO list) includes iconography design, but the rules should describe the icons even before final art is ready.

**Remedy:** Add textual descriptions: "Wrench (a tool icon), Coin (currency symbol), Propeller (spinning blade), Any (star or wildcard icon)."

**Priority:** Moderate — readability before components exist.

---

### Loan Income Reduction vs. Bankruptcy Sequence

**Location:** Section 5.3 (Loans)

**Problem:** The rule states "If forced below −10 during Income & Cleanup, you are bankrupt." But the loan section says you "cannot take" a loan that would push below −10.

What forces you below −10 if you can't voluntarily take such a loan? Is it the Income Track minus Engineers calculation going further negative?

**Remedy:** Clarify: "If your net income during Income & Cleanup would reduce your Income Track below −10 (after mandatory loan-taking to cover negative income), you are bankrupt."

**Priority:** Moderate — edge case needs clarification.

---

### Officer Income Track Missing from Setup

**Location:** Section 3.2 (Player Setup)

**Problem:** Setup lists "Officer Income Track set to 0" and "Engineer Income Track set to 1" which is correct. However, the Flight School action (Section 6.6) that increases Officer Income is the ONLY way to increase this track.

The rules could clarify that Officer Income starts at 0, meaning you get NO free officers each round initially.

**Remedy:** Add clarification: "At 0, you receive no free Officers during Income & Cleanup. Invest in Flight School to begin receiving Officers."

**Priority:** Moderate — implicit but worth stating.

## Balance Observations

These require playtesting to confirm but are worth monitoring:

### Germany's Hindenburg Risk May Be Too Punishing

Germany cannot acquire Helium Handling, meaning EVERY Age III Luxury Launch risks a 1-in-20 chance of Catastrophic Explosion triggering game end. With only 3 VP as compensation, this may make Germany non-competitive in Age III Luxury routes.

**Observation:** Monitor whether Germany players avoid Luxury routes entirely or feel forced into high-risk gambles. The +3 VP may need adjustment or Germany may need an alternative path to competitiveness.

### USA Late War Entry May Be Too Restrictive

USA "Cannot acquire war contract until all other players have one." In a 4-player game, this means waiting for 3 other players to complete contracts. If contracts are scarce or other players delay, USA could be locked out of Age II income.

**Observation:** Playtest whether USA falls behind in Age II or if the Helium advantage compensates adequately.

### Engineer Economy Tension

Engineers serve three purposes:
1. Research generation when Revealing (+1 each)
2. Emergency response during Hazard Checks (spend for +1)
3. Upkeep cost (£1 each reduces net income)

This creates interesting tension, but verify the upkeep cost doesn't make engineers feel "too expensive" to keep.

### First Player Advantage Mitigation

The Ministry action grants First Player token, but there's no initial mitigation for the randomly-selected first player advantage. Consider whether first player needs a minor penalty (start with £14 instead of £15) or if the worker placement exclusivity naturally balances this.

### Income Track Ceiling

There's no stated maximum for the Income Track. In a long game with many successful routes, income could theoretically become excessive. Verify whether a cap is needed or if the Progress Track ends games before this matters.

## Positive Notes

These elements work well and should be preserved:

**Core Physics Check Mechanic**
The Lift ≥ Weight constraint is thematically perfect and creates genuine engineering decisions. Simple formula, deep implications.

**Dual-Use Cards**
The decision between using cards for worker placement (immediate action) vs. saving for Reveal (resources) creates meaningful hand management tension. Excellent design.

**Engineer Tension**
The triple-use of Engineers (Research, Emergency, Upkeep) creates a resource that's never fully comfortable—exactly the right level of scarcity.

**Age Transitions**
Losing upgrades but keeping technologies makes thematic sense (knowledge persists, but physical ships are scrapped) and creates meaningful progression.

**Hazard Deck Personal Ownership**
Each player having their own Hazard Deck with identical contents means players can track probabilities. Catastrophic Explosion being a known 1-in-20 risk adds meaningful tension without pure chaos.

**Faction Theme Integration**
The faction abilities tie well to historical realities (Germany's hydrogen reliance, USA's helium monopoly, Britain's imperial network, Italy's semi-rigid innovations).

**Progress Track End Condition**
Tying game length to Technology acquisition creates a self-regulating end condition where pursuing technologies advances the clock—elegant.
